Mangovita™ Kids Syrup

L-Lysine HCl, L-Arginine, L-Carnitine, Choline Bitartrate, Taurine, Zinc Gluconate & Multivitamins

Mangovita™ Kids is an advanced paediatric health supplement formulated to support healthy growth milestones in growing children. With a rich mango flavour that kids genuinely love, it combines essential amino acids, brain-supporting choline, immune-protective zinc, and active B-complex vitamins without added sugar.

Core Physiological Benefits for Children

  • Supports Growth & Strength: Builds stronger bones and lean muscle tissue with L-Lysine HCl (375 mg) and L-Arginine (100 mg).
  • Appetite Stimulation (भूख बढ़ाने वाला): Enhances hunger and digestive assimilation in picky or reluctant eaters.
  • Enhances Energy & Stamina: L-Carnitine & Taurine power sustained physical stamina throughout school and outdoor play.
  • Mental Alertness & Learning: Choline Bitartrate promotes memory formation, mental alertness, and cognitive acuity.
  • Helps Fight Infections: Therapeutic Zinc Gluconate guards against seasonal respiratory challenges and viral susceptibility.

Recommended Dosage & Directions

Dosage: As recommended by the Healthcare Professional or Nutritionist.
Shake well before each use. Measuring cup provided inside package for exact administration.

Storage Instructions

Store in a cool, dry and hygienic place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep bottle tightly closed after use. Keep out of reach of children.
