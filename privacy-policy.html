Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Kenas Labs, New Delhi, India

At Kenas Labs, we respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting any personally identifiable information you may provide through our website (kenaslabs.com) or direct communications via WhatsApp and email.

When you submit an enquiry, order request, or contact form, we collect the details you voluntarily furnish, including your name, telephone/WhatsApp contact number, city, and any specific questions or quantity preferences.
