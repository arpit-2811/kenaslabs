Our Story & Principles

Formulating a Healthier, Stronger Tomorrow

Kenas Labs was founded on an unyielding principle: daily wellness supplements should be clinically meaningful, 100% sugar-free, and accessible to every family.

Mission & Philosophy

Bridging the Modern Micronutrient Void

In an era of ultra-processed meals, academic stress on children, and demanding work routines for adults, meeting daily nutritional requirements through diet alone has become an uphill battle.

Too many commercial syrups mask low-potency ingredients behind excessive refined sugars and synthetic additives. At Kenas Labs, we set out to build health supplements that respect human physiology: sugar-free, fortified with bioactive amino acids (like L-Lysine, L-Carnitine, and Taurine), and infused with a delicious natural mango flavour that requires zero coaxing to consume.
